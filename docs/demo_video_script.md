# EOIP Demo Video Script

## 1. Objective

This 90-second demo script is designed to present the Energy Operations Intelligence Platform as a credible, evidence-aware operational intelligence system for renewable asset management. The script follows the project’s actual dashboard narrative and highlights the platform’s portfolio overview, plant performance, equipment risk, event context, and recommendation flow without overstating the model claims.

---

## 2. Story Arc

The demo should tell a simple story:

1. the platform gives a portfolio-level view of operational performance
2. a plant issue is narrowed down to a specific operating context
3. the user sees event and equipment signals that explain the issue
4. the platform surfaces analytics and recommendations to support action prioritization
5. the demo closes on disciplined, evidence-aware decision support rather than unsupported certainty

---

## 3. Script (90 seconds)

### Opening (0:00–0:15)

“Here is the Energy Operations Intelligence Platform, an operational intelligence dashboard designed to help teams monitor renewable asset performance, identify operational risks, and support decision-making with structured evidence.”

“Rather than treating data as isolated metrics, the platform brings together plant performance, equipment context, alarms, incidents, forecast signals, and recommendation workflows into a single decision-support view.”

### Portfolio overview (0:15–0:30)

“We begin with the portfolio overview, where the user can compare generation and expected output across plants and quickly identify where attention is needed.”

“This view gives a leadership-level operational picture: which plants are underperforming, which regions need attention, and which issues require a deeper review.”

### Plant and asset drilldown (0:30–0:50)

“From there, we move into plant performance and asset-level detail. The user can inspect the specific operating context behind an issue and review which equipment is contributing to underperformance or risk.”

“The platform also surfaces event context, including alarms and incidents, so the user can understand whether the signal is part of a broader operational pattern or a localized asset concern.”

### Analytics and recommendations (0:50–1:15)

“The intelligence layer adds forecast information, anomaly indicators, and maintenance visibility, helping teams assess risk and prioritize intervention.”

“The recommendation center then organizes these signals into a structured action view, showing why a recommendation is being proposed, what evidence is available, and what operational context supports the recommendation.”

### Closing (1:15–1:30)

“The key point is that EOIP is designed as an evidence-aware operations platform: it provides a structured, transparent operational picture, while clearly separating model output from confirmed failure, financial guarantee, or final root cause.”

“It is a strong foundation for renewable operations intelligence, with clean architecture, traceable decision support, and honest reporting of what the evidence does and does not support.”

---

## 4. Voiceover Tips

- Keep the tone professional, confident, and measured.
- Emphasize “operational intelligence” and “decision support,” not “fully autonomous control.”
- Use precise phrases such as “signals,” “context,” “evidence,” and “review,” which align with the project’s governance posture.
- Avoid implying that predictions are guaranteed outcomes.

---

## 5. Visual Guidance

Suggested sequence of show-on-screen pages:

1. Overview
2. Plant Performance
3. Asset Dashboard
4. Alarms & Incidents
5. Forecast
6. Anomaly
7. Maintenance
8. Recommendation Center

This sequence aligns with the project’s narrative and is easy for stakeholders to follow.

---

## 6. Final Delivery Note

This demo should feel like a concise executive walkthrough of a decision-support platform rather than a marketing animation. The main message is that EOIP brings together operational data, analytics, and recommendation workflows in a transparent and disciplined way, while preserving a clear distinction between signal and confirmed fact.
